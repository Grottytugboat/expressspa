01

Dimensions come first

A swim spa needs its own transport assessment. Send the exact length, width, height and empty weight from the manufacturer, along with the model and pickup location. A standard spa quote is not a reliable guide for an oversized load.

02

Plan the final placement

Truck access, overhead clearance and the distance from the unloading position to the final base all affect the lift plan. Photos are a starting point; a site assessment or additional lifting arrangements may be required.

03

Coordinate with your supplier

If the spa is still at the factory or retailer, tell us when it will be ready for collection. We coordinate the agreed pickup, transport and arrival window after the route and delivery method are confirmed.

Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Can you deliver my swim spa over a fence?

Possibly. Suitability depends on the spa weight, lift reach, truck position and site conditions. Send photos and specifications so we can assess the lifting requirements.

Do you offer interstate swim spa transport?

Selected routes can be arranged across SA, NSW and Southern Queensland. Availability, timing and equipment are confirmed for each job.
