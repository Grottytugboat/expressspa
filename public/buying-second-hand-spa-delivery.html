Buying a second-hand spa? Check the move first.

A practical checklist before arranging collection.

01

Get the access photos

Ask for photos of the spa, the route out of the property, gates and the truck’s loading position. Take the same photos at your home. Access can affect whether the move is feasible and how much it costs.

02

Find the specifications

The model, dimensions and empty weight help determine equipment and transport. If the seller cannot provide them, photograph the identification plate and share any manufacturer information you have.

03

Confirm condition separately

A transport quote is not a condition report. Check working order with the seller and arrange any professional inspection you need before purchase. A low purchase price does not establish the cost of moving or preparing the spa.

04

Agree collection after transport

Confirm the move date and scope before committing to a seller’s collection deadline. Arrange drainage and licensed electrical disconnection where needed; tell your mover what remains to be done.
