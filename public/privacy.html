Your information

Privacy.

01

Information you choose to send

Our quote form collects your name, phone number, pickup and delivery suburbs, optional email, move details and optional access photos. Do not include identity documents, payment details or unnecessary private information in photos.

02

How we use it

We use your details to assess and respond to your enquiry, discuss a quote and arrange a move if you proceed. Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing. If you give an email address, we send an acknowledgement.

03

Storage and service providers

Enquiries and access photos are saved in private Vercel storage in Sydney and emailed through Resend to Mark at Express Spa. Email processing may occur outside Australia. We keep enquiries for handling the request and related business records, and review retained information periodically.

04

Access and removal

Contact mark@mrspa.com.au to ask about information you supplied or request correction or deletion. Some records may need to be retained for an agreed job or business recordkeeping. Website enquiries are not published publicly.
