01

The whole move starts with access

Tell us where your spa is now and where it needs to go. A clear path, the empty spa weight and a few photos help us choose the right equipment and identify anything that needs arranging before delivery day.

02

Collection, transport and placement

We assess loading and unloading separately. A simple pickup can still have a difficult delivery, so we check gates, steps, slopes, fences and the position of the truck before confirming the scope.

03

Know what to arrange before moving

The spa needs to be drained and ready for transport. Any electrical disconnection or reconnection must be arranged with a licensed electrician. Confirm the destination base and any access changes before booking.

Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Can you move a spa within the same property?

Yes, send photos of its current position and proposed location. Even a short move can need specialist equipment or a lift; we will assess it before quoting.

What does a spa relocation quote include?

Your quote sets out the agreed transport, lifting and labour. Let us know about electrical work, preparation or disposal needs so these can be discussed separately.
