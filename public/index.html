Before you book

A few good questions.

How much will my spa move cost?

The quote is based on truck kilometres, crane lifting and labour. Send the route, spa size and access photos so we can confirm the scope and price.

Can you get a spa over a fence?

Our light crane truck can help with suitable lifts. Spa weight, lift distance and site conditions must be assessed before we confirm the method.

Can you collect a spa I bought second-hand?

Yes. Get a transport quote before committing to a seller’s collection deadline, and send access details at both properties.

Does transport include electrical connection?

No electrical work is assumed in your transport quote. Arrange any disconnection or reconnection with a licensed electrician and confirm the agreed scope before booking.
