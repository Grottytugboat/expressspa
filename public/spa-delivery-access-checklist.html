Will a spa fit? Your delivery access checklist.

The measurements and photos that help plan a safe delivery.

01

Measure more than the gate

Measure the narrowest points along the complete access route. Include turns, steps, gradients, overhead clearance and the final placement area. The spa may need clearance for handling equipment as well as its own dimensions.

02

Show the truck position

Take photos from the street towards the property. A crane needs an assessed operating position and load plan. Fence height alone does not tell us whether a lift is possible.

03

Check overhead and ground conditions

Show trees, eaves, cables and any soft or uneven ground. Do not remove obstacles or arrange structural changes until the delivery method is agreed. Powerline or lifting concerns require a proper site assessment.

04

Send a simple photo sequence

Send the spa, street approach and access route at both properties. Add approximate measurements and manufacturer specifications. We will tell you if more detail or an assessment is needed.
